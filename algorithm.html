Preterm <32 weeks — Golden Hour
Team pre-assembled BEFORE delivery
▼
  • 1Wrap immediately in polyethylene plastic bag/wrap — do NOT dry first. Neck down.
  • 2Polyethylene hat on. Pre-heated warmer + thermal mattress.
  • 3FiO₂ 21–30%. Titrate strictly to SpO₂ target.
  • 4SpO₂ probe RIGHT hand immediately.
  • 5Handle gently. Head midline. Avoid rapid volume boluses.
⚠ Avoid SpO₂ > 95% in <32w. Hyperoxia causes retinopathy and oxidative injury. Reduce FiO₂ immediately if above target.

Apply MASK when PPV is NOT effective — no chest rise and/or HR not improving. Work through steps in order before escalating to intubation.
M
Mask — Recheck seal & position
Press thenar eminence down on mask rim. Lift chin into mask. Check for leak at nose bridge. Ensure chin, mouth, nose covered — NOT eyes. Reapply firmly.
A
Airway — Reposition & clear
Reposition in sniffing position (tragus → anterior axillary line). Brief suction if secretions visible. Open mouth slightly with finger under chin.
S
Step up pressure
Increase PIP by 5 cmH₂O increments. Call out new pressure. May go to 30–40 cmH₂O for initial inflation if no chest movement. Confirm on manometer.
K
Key alternative airway
No chest rise despite M-A-S → consider:
LMA size 1 if ≥34 weeks + >1500 g
ETT intubation if LMA not available or fails
After >2 min PPV: Insert OG tube 5F or 6F. Measure: nasal bridge → earlobe → midway between xiphisternum and umbilicus. Leave on open drainage or aspirate with 20 mL syringe.
Apply DOPE for sudden deterioration in any intubated neonate — during resuscitation, stabilisation, or transport. Work through all four causes.
D
Displaced ETT
Check lip depth mark. Auscultate bilateral axillae.
Reduced left = right mainstem → withdraw 0.5–1 cm.
No air entry = extubation → re-intubate.
O
Obstructed ETT
Pass suction catheter through ETT. Resistance or blockage = secretion plug or kinked tube. Suction; replace ETT if cannot clear.
P
Pneumothorax
Signs: asymmetric chest, hyper-resonance, displaced apex. Tension → do NOT wait for CXR.
Emergency decompression: 23G butterfly needle, 2nd ICS, mid-clavicular line, affected side. Aspirate air. Leave in situ until chest drain inserted.
E
Equipment failure
Check all ventilator circuit connections, gas supply, flow meter, PEEP valve integrity, O₂ blender. Replace faulty components. Revert to BVM if ventilator fails.
